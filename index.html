The operating principle

A vision system never fails in the lab. It fails on the customer's lighting, their part variation, their line speed. An AI agent never fails in the demo — it fails on real conversations. Technology succeeds when it changes the real outcome, exposes its own limits, and leaves behind something the customer can trust without taking my word for it.

SYSTEM 01

Signal Validation & Real-Time Market Data Platform

Python · Polygon & Tastytrade APIs · OAuth · Statistical simulation

Challenge

The scanner was finding real directional signal — a measurable lift over a random baseline, enough to feel like an edge. Nothing in the pipeline tested whether that lift survived what it actually costs to trade it.

System

Multi-source ingestion and normalization across three market-data providers, real-time scanning and scoring, automated token refresh and session management, running on an unattended daily cycle with failure sentinels.

Evidence

I built the layer that audited my own system: random-baseline comparison, bootstrap confidence intervals, Cohen’s d, and execution-aware Black-Scholes modeling. The directional lift over random held up. The economics did not — approximately −10.9% modeled option return, roughly a 10% win rate, Kelly fraction 0.00%.

What it shows

A system can predict something real and still fail to produce an economically useful decision. I instrument the thing that tells the difference — and I report the result when it’s the one I didn’t want.

SYSTEM 02

FR8 Boss — Freight Decision-Support Engine

Python · CLI workflows · YAML scenarios · Business rules · Explainable output

Challenge

Load decisions get made in minutes across deadhead, timing, capacity, insurance, backhaul, and margin. Judgment varies by dispatcher and by hour, and the losses stay invisible because nobody logs the reasoning.

System

A decision-support workspace evaluating load economics against repositioning cost, revenue per mile, capacity, insurance constraints, backhaul alternatives, time decay, and decision deadlines.

Evidence

Converts unstructured operating inputs into PASS / WARN / INTERVENE recommendations with the reasoning written out and a reusable audit record. The operator keeps the final call.

What it shows

Map the real workflow before automating it. Make the machine's reasoning inspectable, and leave behind an audit trail the customer can argue with.

SYSTEM 03

Mulligan Made — Computer-Vision Swing Analysis

MediaPipe · OpenCV · Signal processing · FFmpeg · Media & PDF generation

Challenge

Raw video is not feedback and pose data is not coaching. The distance between a working model and something a customer can act on is most of the work.

System

End-to-end pipeline: swing-phase detection, pose-data smoothing, tempo and biomechanics measurement, then translation of motion data into plain-language guidance.

Evidence

Produces annotated replay video, motion overlays, heatmaps, PDF summaries, and QR-linked deliverables — upload to finished artifact with no manual steps in between.

What it shows

Model output is not the deliverable. The last mile is where deployments succeed or fail.

How I approach an AI deployment

The same sequence whether the technology is a vision sensor or an LLM agent.

Find the decision that actually changes — not the workflow that's easiest to automate.
Define success before building, in the customer's numbers, signed off by the person who owns them.
Write down what result would mean this failed — while nobody is attached to it yet.
Build the smallest useful implementation against real data and real edge cases.
Instrument the business outcome, not the model metric. Measure the tail, not the mean.
Turn what worked into a playbook someone else can run without me.
