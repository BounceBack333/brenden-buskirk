# Racing brief (direction received 2026-11-11)

`MASTER_BRIEF.md` is the founder's original charter and still governs conduct: evidence, honesty about status, fairness, synthetic characters, authorisation before spending or publishing. This note records the change of direction that followed it. It is a summary of what the founder passed on, not a transcript.

## What happened

The founder recorded the first build (Catapult Courier) and had it reviewed. Two things came back.

**About that build.** Liked: the world, the role reversal, the catch. Problems: characters too small to read; the camera should be more deliberate; cause and effect should be clearer; first use needs teaching; there should be one convincing "this is you and your dog" character; all-or-nothing scoring is a worry; and the preview's viewer takes screen space, so test a direct, full-screen presentation.

**About direction, which supersedes the rest.** The build drifted from what excited the founder. Make **racing** the core of the next prototype, with **adult friend groups** as the primary audience. Keep personalised people, pets and miniature worlds. Preserve the current build; Courier can stay as a parked experiment.

## The brief for the racing build ("keep it tight")

1. One short track, about a 60–90 second race.
2. One vehicle handling model: steering, grip, sliding, contact, recovery.
3. Simple touch controls: steering and one satisfying manoeuvre, for example a drift that earns a burst of speed.
4. A meaningful route choice: one dependable route and one faster route that needs better execution.
5. Two real players in the first social test.
6. Fast recovery and rematching.
7. Existing characters, used provisionally.

First course idea: "Backyard After Dark" — patio, grass edges, a banked garden hose, a narrow plank shortcut, string lights, a work light. Then one distinctive world interaction: for example a plank shifts into a shortcut that stays available to everyone for the rest of the race.

Characters should read less baby-like over time. Use the starting grid, portraits, reactions and the finish to show drivers' faces.

## The test

> Can you and a friend identify something you did better, get competitive about the result, and choose another race without being prompted?
