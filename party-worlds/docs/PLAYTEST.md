# Observation plan (Stage E preparation)

Nothing in this file has happened yet. No observations are recorded here until real people have played. Do not arrange sessions with anyone outside the founder's own household or friends without the founder's say-so.

## What we are trying to learn, in order

1. **Is launch-and-catch fun for both people?** (the largest open question)
2. Can two first-timers start and finish a round with no verbal help?
3. Do they understand why a delivery scored or failed?
4. Do they choose to play again without being asked?
5. Does seeing "themselves" (or their pet) matter to them?

## Sessions

| Pair | Why |
|---|---|
| Adult + child (roughly 6–10), parent present and in charge | Mixed skill; reading-free comprehension |
| Two adults who play games | Mastery, rivalry inside co-op |
| Two adults who rarely play | First-touch usability |
| One person alone with Buddy | Solo value; is the bot a good partner |

10–15 minutes each, one phone in landscape (same-screen mode) or one phone solo. Say only: *"This is an early game. Play it however you like. I'm testing the game, not you."* Then stay quiet.

## Watch for (tick or tally; write exact words people say)

| Moment | What to note |
|---|---|
| Home → first launch | Seconds elapsed. Did they open Characters first? |
| First pull | Seconds until the first successful launch. Any false starts? |
| First catch attempt | Did the catcher move before release (reading the bend) or only after? |
| Umbrella | Did they find it? Launch number when first used. |
| Dive | Did they find it? Button, tap, or second finger? |
| After a miss | Who do they look at? Laugh, shrug, blame, confusion? Can they say why it missed? |
| Role swap | Any confusion about whose turn it is, or which half of the screen is theirs? |
| Donut | Did anyone aim for it on purpose? Did they say so out loud first? |
| Results | Do they read the breakdown? First button pressed? |
| Rematch | Unprompted "again"? How many rounds before they stop? Who stops? |
| The waiting player | During the other's aim, is the catcher engaged or idle? |
| Characters | Any "that's me / that's the dog" reaction? Do they customise? |

## Afterwards (three questions, then stop)
1. "What was happening in that game?" (their words reveal the model in their head)
2. "Was there a moment you'd show someone?"
3. "Was one job better than the other?"

## What counts as a signal
- **Behaviour over politeness:** an unprompted third round outweighs "that was nice".
- If the catcher is idle through most aim phases in most sessions → the catcher needs a real job during aiming.
- If more than one pair in four cannot launch within 20 s → the pull gesture or its hint fails.
- If nobody uses the umbrella by launch four → it is undiscoverable or unnecessary.

## Record
For each session: date, pair type, device, build label, rounds played, scores, the tallies above, verbatim quotes. Store notes without names or photos. Results go in `PROGRESS.md` under "Observed with real players" — a heading that does not exist until there is something true to put under it.
