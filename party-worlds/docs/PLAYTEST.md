# Observation plan

Nothing in this file has happened yet. No observations are recorded here until real people have played. Do not arrange sessions with anyone outside the founder's own household or friends without the founder's say-so.

This plan is for the racing prototype (Backyard Dash). The plan written for Catapult Courier is in the repository history; that build is parked (D-016).

## The one question

**Can you and a friend each name something you did better, get competitive about the result, and choose another race without being prompted?**

Everything below is there to explain a yes or a no.

## What we are trying to learn, in order

1. Do two friends ask for a second race? A third?
2. After a race, can each say what they did well or badly, in their own words?
3. Does anybody find the drift without help? Does it feel good when they do?
4. Is the plank an argument ("you open it", "don't you dare")? Or does nobody notice it?
5. Can a first-time player stay on the patio?
6. Does seeing themselves or their pet in the kart matter to them?
7. Does it read as a game for adults?

## Sessions

| Who | Set-up | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The founder and one friend | **Split screen** on a tablet or a laptop (keyboard: A/D to steer and W to drift; ←/→ to steer and ↑ to drift) | The test this build was made for |
| The founder alone, on a phone | Race against Gentle rivals, then Keen | First-touch feel of thumb steering and the drift button; is the phone screen enough |
| Two friends who do not play games much | Split screen | Whether steering alone is enough to enjoy it |
| Two friends who do | Split screen, then each solo against Fierce | Whether there is anything to get good at |

About ten minutes each. Say only: *"This is an early game. Play it however you like. I'm testing the game, not you."* Then stay quiet. Do not explain drifting or the plank.

## Watch for (tick or tally; write down exact words)

| Moment | What to note |
|---|---|
| Home → first race | Seconds elapsed. Did they open Characters first? Did they change the rivals? |
| Countdown | Any reaction to meeting their driver face to face? Did anyone try the start boost? |
| First lap | Time off the road. Did they hit the hose? Any "which one am I"? |
| Drift | Race number and lap when first used on purpose. Button, second finger, or by steering hard? First words after the first boost |
| The peg | Who opens the plank, on which lap, and whether it was on purpose. What the other one says |
| The plank | Who takes it. Who falls off. Whether they try again |
| Each other | Talking during the race? Blocking, shoving, towing on purpose? |
| Finish | Do they look at the results or at each other? Who speaks first and what do they say |
| Results | Do they read times and gaps? Do they mention best lap? First button pressed |
| Rematch | Unprompted? How many races before they stop, and who stops |
| Solo | After a win on Gentle, do they take the offer of Keen? |
| Drivers | Who do they pick, and how fast? A grown-up, a pet, or one of the not-people? Any laugh, any "that's you"? Do they build one? Any "this looks like a kids' game" |
| The score | In split screen, do they play the series out to three? Does the loser start a second? Does anyone quote the scoreboard at the other |
| Medals | Alone, do they go again for the next medal? Do they notice the gap |
| Trouble | Rotating the phone, thumbs covering the kart, the split being too small, sound, a palm or a second finger landing on the screen, anyone trying to drive across the lawn |

## Afterwards (three questions, then stop)

1. "What happened in that last race?" (their words show what they understood)
2. "What would you do differently next time?"
3. "What was missing?"

## What counts as a signal

- **Behaviour over politeness:** an unprompted third race outweighs "that was fun".
- If neither player drifts on purpose by the end of race two → the hint and the button are failing.
- If nobody touches the peg in three races → the plank is not being read as a choice.
- If a first-timer spends more of lap one on the grass than on the patio, on Gentle → handling is too fast or too twitchy for this audience (see D-020 for what was tried).
- If they play once and put it down → the core is not there yet, whatever they say.

## Record

For each session: date, who (by role, not name), device, build label, races played, results, the tallies above, verbatim quotes. Store notes without names or photos. Results go in `PROGRESS.md` under "Observed with real players" — a heading that does not exist until there is something true to put under it.
